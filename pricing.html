Honest pricing

Free, because it's a learning project

Domain Toolkit is a personal project, not a commercial product — there's no paid tier, no credit card, and no billing system.

Everything is free

DNS records, propagation checks, health checks, and WHOIS lookups all run on free public infrastructure (Google/Cloudflare DNS-over-HTTPS and a small serverless function). There are no usage limits enforced by this app itself, though the free services it depends on may rate-limit heavy use.
